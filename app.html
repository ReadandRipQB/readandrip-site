The Read & Rip app

The reps for the days you're not with me.

I built an app so quarterbacks can rep coverage recognition and pre-snap reads on their phone, between sessions.

Open the app See training
Why it exists

An hour a week isn't the problem. The other six days are.

A quarterback can learn a coverage on a Tuesday and lose it by Friday, because there's nowhere to practice reading a defense alone. You can throw in the backyard. You can't run Cover 3 in it.

That's the gap the app fills: looks on a screen, over and over, until recognizing them stops being work.

What's inside

Learn it, then prove it

Season mode

Play through a season against different teams. Every snap you call the shell, the coverage, whether a blitz is coming and the box count — then find out if you were right.

Vision Trainer

First person, behind center. You read the look and call the coverage and where the ball should go, before the snap.

Pre-snap read training

Safeties, leverage and depth — what to check before the ball moves.

Coverage modules

Each coverage on its own: what it takes away, and how it fails.

Fronts

Reading the defensive front and what it tells you about the coverage behind it.

Coverage beaters

The concepts that attack each coverage, and why they work.

Beginner's guide

For players who have never been taught any of this. Start here, nothing assumed.

Quizzes

Answer under pressure instead of nodding along. This is where you find out what stuck.

Progress tracking

See what's improving and what keeps getting missed.

Who it's for

Anyone who has to read a defense

  • Quarterbacks at any level, youth through varsity
  • Players new to coverages who need somewhere to start
  • Any position that has to recognize what the defense is in
  • Coaches who want their players studying the same language
