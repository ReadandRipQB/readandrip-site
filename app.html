What's inside

Learn it, then prove it

Start with the basics, go as deep as varsity, and see what's sticking along the way.

01 · Learn

Start from zero. Go as deep as varsity.

Never been taught any of this? The beginner's guide assumes nothing. Then go module by module.

  • Beginner's guideField, positions, lingo and zones. Start here, nothing assumed.
  • Pre-snap readsSafeties, leverage and depth: what to check before the ball moves.
  • Fronts & coveragesWhat each coverage takes away and how it fails, and what the front tells you.
  • Coverage beatersThe concepts that attack each coverage, and why they work.
02 · Read

Vision Trainer

First person, behind center in a 3D stadium. You read the look and call the coverage and where the ball should go, before the snap. Then you see whether you got the read.

03 · Prove

Season Mode & quizzes

Play through a season. On each snap you call the shell and the coverage, then find out if you were right and move the chains.

  • QuizzesCheck what you know instead of nodding along, including a timed two-minute drill.
04 · Track

See what's sticking

See what's improving, what keeps getting missed and your daily streak, so the next rep goes where it matters.

  • Film studyLessons on studying film, plus your own opponent journal for notes and charting.

FAQ

Questions players and parents ask

Do I need to train with you to use the app?+
No. Anyone can use it: players, parents and coaches. Start free in any browser or on Google Play.
Is it only for quarterbacks?+
Quarterbacks get the most out of it, but any player who has to recognize coverage will find it useful, and coaches use it too.
What ages is it for?+
There is a beginner's guide for players new to coverages, and the modules go deep enough for varsity quarterbacks.
