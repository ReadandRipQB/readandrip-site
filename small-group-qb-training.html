Why a group works

Some things only happen with someone next to you

Competitive reps

A throw in front of a teammate isn't the same throw as one taken alone. QBs sharpen up when somebody is watching and going next.

One language

When your QB room hears the same terms from the same coach, they start talking to each other about coverage instead of guessing separately.

Same hour, less money

$50 per QB instead of $75. Same field, same coach, same 60 minutes.

What the hour looks like

The 60 minutes

0–10 minWarm-up & footworkEverybody moving at once. No lines.
10–25 minMechanicsOne fix per QB, because they don't all need the same one.
25–45 minReadsSame look on the board, each QB calls it out loud. This is where a group beats a private hour.
45–55 minCompetitive repsSituational throws on a clock, with everyone watching.
55–60 minRecapEach QB leaves with one rule of their own, not a shared list.

FAQ

Before you book a group

How many QBs train together?+
Two or three. Small enough that every QB gets full reps and nobody stands in line.
What if nobody else books my time slot?+
Small groups need a minimum of two QBs. If you end up as the only athlete in your slot, the session runs as a full private one-on-one at the $75 rate instead, and the $25 difference is due at the session.
Do the QBs have to be the same age or level?+
No. Everyone reads the same look; what changes is how far I go with each QB on it. A varsity starter and the backup behind him get a lot out of the same hour.
Can we bring QBs from different schools?+
Yes. Teammates, friends from other programs, or a couple of QBs who just want the reps.
Where do we meet?+
Brighton, Michigan. I text the exact location before your session.
How do I pay, and what if we have to cancel?+
Payment is due at booking. Reschedule free with 24 hours' notice; cancel with 24 hours' notice for a full refund. If weather forces me to call off an outdoor session, you choose a free reschedule or a full refund.
