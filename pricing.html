Which plan?

A quick guide

I'm a player or a parent+
Start free. If you want every module, the full drill library and the complete quiz bank, upgrade to Pro. Yearly works out cheaper than monthly.
I coach one team+
Coach gives you the playbook designer, team playbook, quizzes on your plays and player tracking, for $129 a year or $14.99 a month. Players who already have Pro join free, 2 player seats for players without Pro are included, and more come in blocks of 11 for $20 a year.
I run a school or a whole program+
Program covers multiple teams and up to 10 staff, with 11 players without Pro included and more in blocks of 11 for $20 a year. Book a free 15-minute demo if you want to see it first.
How is QB training paid for?+
Training is booked and paid per session on Calendly. App plans are chosen and paid for inside the app.
