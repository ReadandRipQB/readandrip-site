FAQ

How film reports work

Do we need to be on a call?+
No. Send the film, and the report and video come back to you within 24 hours. There's nothing to attend.
What film should we send?+
One full game. Hudl, YouTube or a phone recording all work. Full-game film beats a highlight tape, because highlights only show the throws that worked.
Does this work if we're not in Michigan?+
Yes. Film reports are fully remote.
Can we do it live instead?+
Yes. The live chalk talk is an hour on a call where we watch together and your QB can ask questions as we go.
Who sees the film?+
Only me. Your film is used to coach your QB and nothing else.
