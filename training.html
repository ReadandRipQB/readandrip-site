FAQ

Questions parents ask

Where do sessions take place?+
Sessions are in Brighton, Michigan. You'll get the field location when you book. I regularly work with families from Brighton, Howell, Hartland, Pinckney, South Lyon, Milford, Whitmore Lake, Fenton, Novi and Northville.
Is my QB too young?+
I train QBs from youth football through college. Younger players start with mechanics and simple pre-snap keys.
Do you train flag football QBs?+
Yes. Girls and youth flag QBs get training built around 5-on-5 reads.
What should my QB bring?+
Cleats, water, and a football if they have a favorite. I bring everything else.
How does a film report work if we're not local?+
Film reports are fully remote. Send a link to one game and you'll get the report and video back within 24 hours.
What does quarterback training cost?+
One-on-one training is $75 for 60 minutes. Small-group training is $50 per QB. A film report is $50 per game. No packages and no contracts.
