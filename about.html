About Coach Zach

Zach Trainor

I played quarterback at the D1, D2 and D3 levels. Now I train QBs full time in Brighton, Michigan, from 8-year-olds to varsity starters, tackle and flag.

Played quarterback at
D1 · D2 · D3
Where it started

Walled Lake Western

I played high school football at Walled Lake Western, where I was named All-State, made the Dream Team, and was a Mr. Football candidate.

From there I played quarterback at the D1, D2 and D3 levels — three programs, three systems, and a lot of different coaches with very different standards for what a quarterback is supposed to know. That range is what I teach from now.

Who taught me

Both sides of the ball

Kurt Warner

I've trained with Kurt Warner — Super Bowl champion, NFL MVP and Hall of Famer — and a lot of how I teach the position traces back to that work.

Steve Wilson

I learned the defensive side from Steve Wilson, who played nine NFL seasons at defensive back for the Cowboys and Broncos, started at cornerback in Super Bowl XXII, and was head coach at Howard University for 13 years.

Studying offense extensively and then being taught defense by someone who coached it at that level is why my training starts with what the defense is doing. You can't beat a picture you can't read.

College camps

I know what college coaches look for.

I've been blessed to travel across the country and compete at college camps against some of the best quarterbacks in the country. I know how those evaluations work, what coaches are actually watching for, and what separates the QBs who get looks from the ones who don't.

If your QB is heading toward that process, it's worth understanding before you're standing on the field.
