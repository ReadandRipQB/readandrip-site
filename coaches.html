Coaching tools

Build it once. Your players see it.

Everything lives on the coach side; your players see it on theirs.

Build

Plays, systems and game plans

  • Playbook designerDraw your own plays or start from the pre-built play library.
  • System builderPut together a full offensive system from the library or your own plays.
  • Call sheet designerBuild game-plan call sheets and print-ready wristbands from your plays.
  • Practice plan designerScript practice by period and position.
Share & teach

Teach your system, not a generic one

Players learn the plays your team actually runs, then prove it on quizzes you build.

  • Team playbookAvailable to every player on the team.
  • Quizzes on your playsBuild quizzes tied to the plays your team runs.
  • Opponent scoutingScout an opponent and share player-facing reports.
  • Film assignmentsAssign film, then read what each player wrote.
  • Goals & announcementsSet team goals and post announcements.
  • Roster & position groupsOrganize players by position group and choose what each one sees.

  • Performance & trendsQuiz and concept-check averages for every player.
