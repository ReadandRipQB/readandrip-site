FAQ

Flag questions

Is flag quarterback play really different?+
Yes. The rush count changes the timing, the field is wider with fewer defenders, and leverage matters more than arm strength.
Do you train whole flag teams?+
Yes. I'll run a session for a program's quarterbacks. Email me and we'll find a practice night.
When is the training window for flag?+
Michigan girls high school flag plays in the spring, which makes fall and winter the best time to train.
What ages do you take for flag?+
Youth through high school, taught at an age-appropriate level.
